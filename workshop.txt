The code, the dropped item models, the item icons and the poster are new. Full credits are at the bottom.

description=Sew capacity upgrades and weight-reduction straps onto your bags.
description=
description=[h2]Why a separate mod[/h2]
description=Dynamic Backpack Upgrades had performance trouble and bag stats that kept reverting, so I rewrote its code from scratch. Before anything else, I want to find out in real games whether the new code is actually better or worse, and a separate mod is the way to do that without touching the original:
description=[list]
description=[*]Dynamic Backpack Upgrades stays exactly as it is, for everyone who uses it.
description=[*]You can try this version on the same save, and go back to Dynamic Backpack Upgrades if it plays worse: both read the same items, settings and upgrade data, so upgraded bags carry over either way.
description=[*]Bug reports and comparisons with the original are very welcome in the comments.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Replaces Dynamic Backpack Upgrades in existing saves[/h2]
description=The upgrade items, recipes and sandbox settings keep their names, and bags you already upgraded keep their upgrades and their exact numbers. Unsubscribe from Dynamic Backpack Upgrades and its Hide & Tarp Fanny Packs extension (now built in) before enabling this mod; the game will not load them together.
description=
description=[h2]How it plays[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]Right-click a bag:[/b] [i]Add Upgrade[/i] lists the upgrade items you carry. [i]Remove Upgrade[/i] cuts one off and gives the item back. Adding takes a sewing needle and one use of thread, and gives Tailoring XP if the server sets some (off by default); removing takes scissors (or a sharp knife, if the sandbox allows it).
description=[*][b]Right-click an upgrade item:[/b] [i]Sew onto Bag[/i] lists the bags in your inventory with their free slots, bags inside other bags included.
description=[*][b]Bag upgrades[/b] (cloth, denim, leather, military) add a share of the bag's original capacity plus a flat amount. [b]Straps[/b] remove a share of the weight the bag does not already reduce.
description=[*][b]Slots:[/b] every container item counts as a bag (backpacks, fanny packs, handbags, toolboxes, wallets, keyrings...). Each has the base slots, plus a modifier for back bags, fanny packs and other bags, plus one slot every few Tailoring levels.
description=[*][b]Crafting:[/b] the upgrade items are sewn from fanny packs (regular, Hide and Tarp), belts, ripped sheets, denim and leather strips, in the Tailoring category.
description=[*][b]Dropped items[/b] have their own models: a padded pouch for each bag upgrade and a padded shoulder strap with its buckle for each straps upgrade, in the material's colours.
description=[*][b]Tooltips[/b] show a bag's slots and what its upgrades add, and what an upgrade item does with the current settings. The game caps a bag at 50 capacity minus its own weight; the tooltip says so when an upgrade goes past it.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]What changed from Dynamic Backpack Upgrades[/h2]
description=[list]
description=[*][b]No more reverting stats:[/b] the server sets a bag's stats once, when an upgrade goes on or comes off, and they are saved with the bag. Nothing re-checks your inventory every minute or every time you hover a bag, and the "Fix Upgrades" menu is gone because stats no longer drift.
description=[*][b]Multiplayer:[/b] the server checks the bag, the upgrade and the tools again before it changes anything, uses nothing up if the check fails, and sends the new stats to your client straight away.
description=[*][b]Crafting:[/b] a fanny pack with anything in it is no longer accepted (the original deleted its contents). Hide and Tarp fanny packs are accepted, and the recipes show their names.
description=[*][b]Key Ring:[/b] the plain Key Ring takes upgrades too (the original banned that one item; the decorated keyrings were already allowed).
description=[*][b]Loot:[/b] the loot multipliers follow the sandbox settings.
description=[*][b]Settings changes:[/b] when the upgrade strength settings change, the bags players carry are updated within ten in-game minutes. Bags lying in the world are updated the next time an upgrade goes on or comes off.
description=[*][b]Sewing[/b] uses the vanilla sewing animation and sound and takes as long as patching clothing, faster with Tailoring.
description=[/list]
description=
description=[h2]Sandbox options (page "Bag Upgrades")[/h2]
description=Same names and defaults as Dynamic Backpack Upgrades: Knives Remove Upgrades (off), Base Upgrade Slots (1), Back Bag / Fanny Pack / Other Bag Slot Modifiers (+1 / 0 / 0), Tailoring Levels per Extra Slot (10), and for each material a capacity share, a flat capacity bonus, a weight reduction share and a loot multiplier. New here: Tailoring XP for Sewing On an Upgrade (0, off by default; patching clothing gives 2).
